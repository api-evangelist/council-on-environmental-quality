First Lady Melania Trump launched her 2027 Presidential Super Intelligence (SI) Challenge and established a new First Lady Seal of SI Readiness—inviting K-12 students, educators, and schools across America to participate in the nationwide initiative. “America’s next groundbreaking idea is already sitting in a classroom,” said Mrs. Trump.

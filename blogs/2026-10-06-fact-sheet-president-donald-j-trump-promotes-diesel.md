ADDRESSING AMERICAN DIESEL AFFORDABILITY: Today, President Donald J. Trump signed an Executive Order to temporarily allow off-road “dyed” diesel for highway use and defer the applicable Federal excise tax, lowering costs for Americans. PUTTING MONEY BACK INTO THE POCKETS OF AMERICANS: President Trump is addressing fuel affordability for American consumers, farmers, truckers, and workers.
